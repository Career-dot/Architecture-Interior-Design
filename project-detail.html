Fig. 01 — Residential

Casa Remás

A full renovation of a 1930s townhouse in Lisbon's Estrela district, reworked around a new central stair and a single skylight that pulls daylight down through all three storeys.

Fig. 02 — The Brief

A dark, subdivided house, opened back up

The house had been split into three separate rental units over the decades, with a narrow internal stair as the only connection between floors. Our clients, a family of four, wanted it returned to a single home without losing the rental unit's separate street entrance, which now serves as a home office.

The existing stair was removed and rebuilt half a metre further back, opening a triple-height void beneath a new steel-framed skylight. Every room on the upper two floors now borrows light from that void rather than relying solely on the street-facing windows.

Material choices stayed close to what was already there: the original chestnut floorboards were lifted, sanded and relaid, and all new joinery uses the same timber to match.
