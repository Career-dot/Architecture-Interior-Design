Our services

01

Architecture

Full architectural service for new-builds and extensions: feasibility and planning strategy, concept and technical design, and construction documentation.

Typical scope: single & multi-family homes, small commercial buildings

02

Interior Design

Spatial planning, material and lighting specification, and bespoke joinery design for homes, offices and hospitality venues, including full FF&E sourcing.

Typical scope: apartments, offices, restaurants, boutique hotels

03

Renovation

Heritage-sensitive retrofits and structural renovations of existing buildings, balancing conservation requirements against modern performance standards.

Typical scope: pre-war apartments, listed façades, adaptive reuse

04

Consultation

Short-form advisory for smaller projects: a single working session to pressure-test a layout, a material palette, or a builder's drawing set before it goes to tender.

Typical scope: single rooms, self-build support, second opinions

Fig. 02 — How a Project Runs

Four phases, start to finish

01

Discover

02

Design

Concept options, then a single developed design signed off with you.

03

Develop

Technical drawings, specifications, and contractor tendering.

04

Deliver

On-site support through construction, snagging, and handover.
